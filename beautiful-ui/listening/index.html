A listening room,
not a streaming service.

These three short compositions are original studies made from layered sine tones. Your browser creates the sound directly. There are no borrowed recordings, remote audio files, or accounts.

Choose a sound to play it. Use the play button to pause, the progress control to move through it, and the volume control to set your level. Each piece ends on its own. Nothing starts automatically.

The volume slider and mute button are available on every screen. Your device volume controls also work.

This is an interface and sound experiment, not a wellness treatment. Return to Furthering Design.
