# Phase 2: explore directions

Generate at least three coherent directions from the validated intent.

Each direction must differ materially across composition, typography, spatial rhythm, surface, imagery, interaction, and voice. A palette, font, or border-radius change does not create a new direction.

For every direction provide:

- one sentence thesis
- user hypothesis
- structural pattern and page sequence
- decision rationale and deliberate tradeoffs
- rejected category conventions
- the evidence that would falsify the direction
- implementation risks
- an immutable baseline reference, allowed paths, protected paths, and diff-only change contract

Do not merge directions during generation. Do not score while generating. Do not copy a reference style. Retrieve references for functional analogy and principles, then create an original composition from product truth.
